Afterglow

Privacy policy

Last updated 15 October 2026 · applies to Afterglow for Android, version 1.0

The short version

Everything you log in Afterglow stays on your phone, encrypted. There is no account, no tracking and no advertising, and nothing you log is ever sent to us.

Who we are

Afterglow is made by AE Developments. Questions about privacy go to ae.development0@gmail.com.

What Afterglow stores, and where

Afterglow stores what you enter yourself: the moments you log (who it was with, date and time, place, country, rating, positions, foreplay, protection, how often you finished and your notes), period days, your lists, the players for truth or dare, and your settings.

All of this is stored only on your phone. It is encrypted with AES-256, and the key is kept in the Android Keystore, so other apps can’t read it. Afterglow has switched off Android’s automatic backup to Google, so your diary isn’t copied to the cloud without you knowing.

If you turn on the app lock, Afterglow stores only a scrambled version (a salted hash) of your PIN, never the PIN itself.

What we receive

Nothing. This version of Afterglow does not send anything you log over the internet. It has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no account. We can’t see your diary, and we can’t get it back for you.

Fingerprint and face unlock

If you unlock Afterglow with your fingerprint or face, Android checks it. Afterglow only hears “yes” or “no” and never receives any biometric data.

Backups you make yourself

You can make a backup file in Settings. It is encrypted with a password you choose, and you decide where to save it, for example Google Drive, your computer or an email to yourself. The service you choose stores the file under its own terms, but without your password nobody can open it, including us.

Other people in your diary

The names you enter about partners stay on your phone, just like everything else. Please treat them with care, and use the “Hide names” setting or nicknames if you like.

Age

Afterglow is meant for adults aged 18 and older only.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to see, correct and delete your personal data. Because we don’t receive or keep your data, everything is in your own hands: you can change or delete any entry in the app, erase everything in Settings, or uninstall the app, which deletes the diary from your phone.

Changes

If a new version changes how data is handled, for example an optional automatic backup, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date at the top.
